## The brief

Kathir Solar Solutions sells a technical, high-consideration product — solar installations that
customers research for weeks before ever picking up the phone. Their old presence didn't match
that: it couldn't explain what they actually install, why it pays for itself, or why a visitor
should trust them over the next search result. They needed a site that could carry that
conversation on its own, before a sales call ever happens.

## Approach

We treated this as an education problem first, a design problem second. Working with the team,
we broke down their product line — residential, commercial, and industrial solar — into clear,
plain-language breakdowns a homeowner or a facilities manager could actually follow, without
losing the technical credibility that commercial buyers look for.

Every page was built around a single next step: get a visitor to request a site consultation.
Product pages, savings explanations, and installation process all lead there, so nobody has to
hunt for a way to get in touch.

## What we built

- A corporate marketing site covering residential, commercial, and industrial solar offerings
- Plain-language explanations of installation, savings, and process, without dumbing down the technical detail
- A consultation-request flow built as the site's primary conversion path

## Outcome

Kathir Solar Solutions launched with a site that does the explaining their sales team used to
have to do from scratch on every first call. [View the live site](https://kathirsolarsolutions.in).

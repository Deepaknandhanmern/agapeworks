## The brief

UCX Group needed a corporate site that could speak to enterprise decision-makers — visitors who
research vendors carefully before ever picking up the phone. The site had to communicate scope
and credibility clearly, without the generic "stock-photo corporate" feel that undermines trust
rather than building it.

## Approach

We treated information architecture as the core design problem: what does a prospective partner
need to understand, in what order, before they're ready to make contact? That shaped a clear
structure — what the company does, how it operates, and a direct path to getting in touch —
rather than a homepage trying to say everything at once.

Visual design followed the same restraint: a professional, confident tone built from typography
and layout rather than decoration, so the site reads as substantial without relying on filler.

## What we built

- A structured corporate site with clear information hierarchy for enterprise visitors
- Consistent, professional visual design across every page
- A straightforward path from landing on the site to making contact

## Outcome

UCX Group now has a site that presents them the way they present themselves to
partners — clearly, credibly, and without noise. [View the live site](https://www.ucx-group.com).
